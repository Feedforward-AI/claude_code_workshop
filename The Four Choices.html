The four choices

On the dimensions of your AI harness — and the decisions still being made on your behalf.

Your company's AI is operating with a harness right now — a set of choices about what it knows, what it can do, how it works, and what stops it from causing harm. Those choices were made by someone. The question is whether you know who, and what they decided.

This short exercise walks you through twelve questions across the four dimensions. It is not a score. It will not tell you what your harness should be. It will tell you which decisions about your AI are worth focusing on next — based on what you say you don't yet know.

Before you start

Answer from the perspective of your company today. "I don't know" is not a wrong answer — it is the most diagnostic one. Some questions allow more than one answer; the form will tell you. Roughly five minutes. Your answers save automatically.
