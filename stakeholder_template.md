# [Name], [Title]

*Last updated: [Date]*

> This file is context about a key person in your work. The more specific and honest you are, the more useful Claude can be when you ask for help preparing for meetings, drafting communications, or thinking through decisions involving this person. Start with the first six sections for your quick version. Fill in the rest over time.

---

## Role and Relationship
*Who are they, and what's your relationship to them? Direct manager? Peer? Internal client? External partner? One or two sentences.*

## What They Care About
*Their enduring values and priorities. What drives their decisions? What do they measure success by? What keeps them up at night? This is the "why" behind everything they do.*

## What's On Their Plate Right Now
*Their current, time-bound priorities. What are they personally focused on this quarter? What are they being held accountable for in the near term?*

## Communication Style
*How do they prefer to receive information? Direct or diplomatic? Details or big picture? Long emails or short bullets? In meetings or in writing? Morning or afternoon?*

## What Makes Them Say Yes
*When do they get enthusiastic? What kinds of proposals, framings, or evidence earn their support?*

## What Makes Them Skeptical
*What patterns or approaches turn them off? What makes them push back or disengage?*

## Decision Authority and Influence
*What can this person actually approve, block, accelerate, or escalate? Where does their authority end and someone else's begin? Who do they defer to, and who defers to them? This is often more useful than personality when you're planning how to get something done.*

## Constraints and Incentives
*What pressures are they operating under right now? Budget, headcount, board pressure, timeline, politics, legal exposure, team capacity, their own boss. Behavior is often better explained by constraints than by character.*

## Current Dynamic With Me
*Where is this relationship right now? Smooth? Tense? Recovering from something? Is there unresolved history that affects how you interact?*

## Relationships With Other Stakeholders
*How do they relate to other key people in your orbit? Who do they trust? Who do they clash with? Whose endorsement do they respect? This is often the highest-value section — it lets Claude navigate the politics, not just the people.*

## What I Need From Them Right Now
*Specific, time-bound. What's the concrete outcome you're working toward with this person in the next few weeks?*

## What They Need From Me
*The implicit expectations — what do they count on you for? Speed? Precision? No surprises? Polished drafts? Early warning? These are the things you only notice when you violate them.*

## Red Flags and Landmines
*Specific topics, people, or history to avoid. Not logical objections — personal sensitivities. "Don't bring up the 2024 reorg." "Don't mention her former team."*

## Recent Context (Rolling Log)
*A running record of recent meetings, promises made, unresolved threads, or fresh tensions. Keep this short — just the last two or three meaningful interactions. Date each entry. This is the field most likely to go stale, so update it when something happens rather than on a schedule.*

*Example format:*
- *[April 4] Met about Q3 budget. Promised to send a revised proposal by Friday. She seemed distracted — unclear if she's fully bought in.*
- *[March 28] Raised the compliance issue in the leadership sync. He pushed back hard. Needs follow-up in private.*
